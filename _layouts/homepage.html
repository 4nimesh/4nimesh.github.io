About Me

Hi there! I am currently an NBHM postdoctoral fellow in the Department of Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru, working under the supervision of Prof. Soumya Das.

I received my Ph.D. from IIT (BHU), Varanasi, where my research was supervised by Dr. Abhash Kumar Jha. My mathematical research primarily centers on the theory of modular forms, exploring properties of Jacobi forms, and automorphic representations.
