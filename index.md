## About

I am an NBHM Postdoctoral Fellow in the [Department of Mathematics](https://math.iisc.ac.in/) at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), working with [Prof. Soumya Das](https://sites.google.com/site/somuhomepage/). My research is in number theory, mainly modular forms and Jacobi forms.

I received my Ph.D. from [IIT (BHU), Varanasi](https://www.iitbhu.ac.in/), under the supervision of [Dr. Abhash Kumar Jha](https://www.iitbhu.ac.in/dept/mat/people/abhashmat). Before that, I completed a B.Sc. in Mathematics (Honours) at [Cooch Behar College](https://coochbeharcollege.ac.in/) and an M.Sc. in Mathematics at the [University of North Bengal](https://nbu.ac.in/).
